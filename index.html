Start here

  • SharePoint siteSharePoint

    A SharePoint site is a website inside Microsoft 365 where a team or organisation keeps its files, news, lists and pages in one place. Think of it as a shared online room: it has its own address, its own furniture (files and lists) and its own guest list (who may come in).

  • Power AppsPower Platform

    Power Apps lets people build business apps without being professional programmers, by placing things on a screen and describing behaviour with simple formulas.

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium)Copilot

    Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant built into Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, Outlook and Teams. You ask it in everyday language and it helps you write, summarise and find things, using your own work information.

  • Hub siteSharePoint

    A hub site links related SharePoint sites together so they feel like one connected area. Picture a department lobby with doors to several rooms: every room shares the same signs and style, and the news from all of them shows up in the lobby.

  • DataversePower Platform

    Dataverse is the place where Power Platform apps keep their data, in organised tables with built-in security. Think of it as a secure, well-labelled filing room.

  • Copilot StudioCopilot

    Copilot Studio is where you build AI assistants, called agents, with little or no code. You describe what you want, give the agent knowledge and tools, then put it where people already work.
