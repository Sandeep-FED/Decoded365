## Beginner
A sensitivity label is a tag on a file saying how confidential it is and how it must be handled.

## Maker
Labels can add protections such as encryption and restrict who can access content.

## Architect
Managed in [[microsoft-purview]]. AI output can inherit or reflect labels, so make sure labels are applied consistently.
