## Beginner
Microsoft Search is the search tool across Microsoft 365 for finding people, files, sites and messages.

## Maker
It returns only what you are allowed to see, and it also feeds Copilot.

## Architect
Results are security-trimmed against the user's identity. See [[organisational-search]] and [[semantic-index]].
