## Beginner
Microsoft Entra ID is the service that knows who your people are and signs them in.

## Maker
It handles sign-ins and access controls across Microsoft 365 and many connected apps, including Copilot. Admins manage users, groups and conditional access there.

## Architect
It issues the tokens that apps and agents use for [[authentication]]. Many older articles use the previous name, Azure Active Directory (Azure AD).
