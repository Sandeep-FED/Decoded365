## Beginner
Input is whatever you give the AI: your question, files, pictures or data.

## Maker
Besides your typed [[prompt]], inputs can include chat history, instructions and retrieved content.

## Architect
Treat all input as potentially untrusted, because content retrieved into a request may carry instructions (see [[prompt-injection]]).
