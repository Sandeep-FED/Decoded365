## Beginner
Harmful content is material that could be unsafe, abusive, hateful or otherwise inappropriate.

## Maker
AI services use safety controls and moderation to detect and limit it in what people ask and what the AI replies.

## Architect
Controls apply to both inputs and outputs. Configure thresholds and review settings for your use case rather than assuming defaults suit it.
