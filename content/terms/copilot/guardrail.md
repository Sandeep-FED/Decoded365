## Beginner
A guardrail is a safety rail that keeps an AI from going where it should not.

## Maker
It can be a filter, a rule or a limit that stops unsafe, inappropriate or out-of-scope behaviour.

## Architect
Guardrails can be technical (filters, allow-lists, [[permission-trimming]]) or procedural ([[policy|policies]], approvals). Layer them, because none is sufficient on its own.
