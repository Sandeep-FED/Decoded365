## Beginner
Copilot in PowerPoint is an AI helper that can build and polish slide decks from your ideas or documents.

## Maker
Use it to create a first draft, reorganise slides, shorten text or write speaker notes. You still edit the story and check the facts.

## Architect
It can use files the user has access to as source material. Brand, accuracy and licensing of images remain the author's responsibility.
