## Beginner
Organisational context is what the AI knows about how your organisation works: roles, relationships, files and activity.

## Maker
It makes answers more relevant, for example by knowing who your team is.

## Architect
It is assembled from work data and respects permissions. See [[user-context]] for the person-specific side.
