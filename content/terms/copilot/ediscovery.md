## Beginner
eDiscovery is how organisations find and collect electronic information for legal or investigation needs.

## Maker
Compliance teams use it to search, hold and review email, files and chats. This can include content created with Copilot, depending on the service.

## Architect
The capability is part of [[microsoft-purview]]. Retention, holds and audit settings decide what can be found, so align them with legal requirements.
