## Beginner
Lifecycle management is looking after an agent from the first idea until it is switched off.

## Maker
That means building, testing, approving, releasing, monitoring, updating and retiring it, in a controlled way.

## Architect
Define owners, review points and retirement criteria. Orphaned agents are a common governance gap.
