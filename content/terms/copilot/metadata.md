## Beginner
Metadata is the "label on the box": facts about a file like its author, date and type.

## Maker
Good metadata makes content easier to find and to control, and AI search uses it.

## Architect
Search and retrieval use metadata for filtering, ranking and security trimming. Consistent metadata improves results.
