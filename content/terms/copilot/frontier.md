## Beginner
Frontier is Microsoft's early-access club: an opt-in programme that lets your organisation try new Microsoft 365 features before they are officially released.

## Maker
It covers both AI and non-AI features. An administrator enrols chosen users in the Microsoft 365 admin centre, under Copilot, then Settings, then Copilot Frontier. Everything in it is a [[preview]], so features can change and may behave differently from finished ones.

## Architect
Frontier experiences are previews with their own terms. For example, they are not covered by the HIPAA business associate agreement, so check compliance needs before enrolling users who handle regulated data. Treat the programme as a controlled way to test ahead of [[general-availability]], not as a production commitment.
