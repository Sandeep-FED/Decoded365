## Beginner
Responsible AI means building and using AI fairly, safely and transparently, with someone accountable.

## Maker
It covers fairness, safety, privacy, security, inclusiveness and accountability.

## Architect
Put principles into practice with assessments, [[evaluation]], documentation and [[oversight]].
