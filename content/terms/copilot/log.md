## Beginner
A log is a written record of what happened, kept so someone can look back later.

## Maker
Logs help with fixing problems, checking security and understanding use.

## Architect
Decide what to log, for how long and who can read it. Logs may hold sensitive information. Compare [[trace|traces]] and the [[unified-audit-log]].
