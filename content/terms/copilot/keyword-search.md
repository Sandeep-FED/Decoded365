## Beginner
Keyword search finds things by matching the exact words you type.

## Maker
It is quick and exact, but it won't find a document that uses different wording for the same idea.

## Architect
Modern systems combine it with [[semantic-search]] in [[hybrid-search]]. Keyword matching remains the best way to find codes, names and exact phrases.
