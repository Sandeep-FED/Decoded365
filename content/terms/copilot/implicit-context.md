## Beginner
Implicit context is the background an app supplies on its own, like the document you have open, without you attaching anything.

## Maker
It saves effort, but it also means the AI may be using something you did not consciously select.

## Architect
The application decides what is included. Understand what is sent so that sensitive content is not exposed unintentionally. See also [[app-context]].
