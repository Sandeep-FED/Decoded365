## Beginner
Reasoning is the AI working out an answer step by step rather than blurting it out.

## Maker
It helps with complex problems and planning, but takes longer.

## Architect
Reasoning models spend extra computation on intermediate steps. Check conclusions, as fluent reasoning can still be wrong.
