## Beginner
Generative AI is AI that creates new things, such as text, images or code, from your instructions.

## Maker
It is what sits behind Copilot's writing and summarising. It makes new content rather than just sorting or labelling existing content.

## Architect
Models generate output probabilistically from learned patterns, which explains both their fluency and the risk of [[hallucination]]. See also [[non-deterministic-output]].
