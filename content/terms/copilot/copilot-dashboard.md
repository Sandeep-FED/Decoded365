## Beginner
The Copilot Dashboard is a report that shows how much Copilot is being used and what difference it makes.

## Maker
Leaders and admins use it to understand adoption, usage patterns and impact on work, using signals available in Microsoft 365.

## Architect
It draws on workplace analytics from [[viva-insights]]. It complements the admin [[usage-report|usage reports]], which are more about activity than outcomes.
