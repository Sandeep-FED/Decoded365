## Beginner
Data minimisation means using only the information you really need for the job, and no more.

## Maker
When connecting AI to data, choose the narrowest set of sources that does the task. That reduces risk if something goes wrong.

## Architect
It is a privacy principle that complements [[least-privilege]]: limit what is collected and exposed, then limit who and what can reach it.
