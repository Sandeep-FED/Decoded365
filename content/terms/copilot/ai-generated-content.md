## Beginner
AI-generated content is anything an AI wrote or made for you: text, images, summaries, code and so on.

## Maker
Treat it as a starting point. It is made from your instructions and whatever context the tool could see, so it can be wrong or miss the point, and you remain responsible for what you send or publish.

## Architect
Because output is produced probabilistically, apply [[verification]] and sensitivity-label rules to it as you would to any other content. Some organisations also track or mark AI-generated material for compliance.
