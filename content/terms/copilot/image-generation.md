## Beginner
Image generation is when AI draws a picture from a written description.

## Maker
It can speed up mockups and illustrations. Check rights, brand fit and accuracy before using the results.

## Architect
Policies on use, labelling and [[protected-material]] apply to generated images as to other [[ai-generated-content]].
