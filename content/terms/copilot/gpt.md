## Beginner
GPT is a family of AI language models, and the name stands for generative pre-trained transformer.

## Maker
Microsoft Copilot experiences may use OpenAI models like these alongside Microsoft's own services and controls.

## Architect
GPT is a model family, not a product. What a user experiences also depends on [[orchestration]], grounding and safety layers.
