## Beginner
Latency is how long you wait between asking and getting the answer.

## Maker
Bigger tasks, long prompts and complex agents take longer.

## Architect
Latency is affected by model size, [[context-window]] use, [[tool-calling|tool calls]] and retrieval. Measure it as part of [[evaluation]].
