## Beginner
Business Chat is an older name for the Copilot chat that could draw on your work information across apps.

## Maker
You may still see the name in older articles and videos. Today's Microsoft 365 Copilot naming has moved on, so use [[microsoft-365-copilot-chat]] and [[microsoft-365-copilot]] for current terms.

## Architect
When you meet the name in older documentation, map it to the cross-app chat experience in the current product, but check what the current product actually offers rather than assuming the features are identical.
