## Beginner
Intent is what you are really trying to achieve, which the AI tries to work out from your words.

## Maker
If you write "my laptop won't start", the intent might be "get IT support".

## Architect
Agents use intent to route to a [[topic]] or [[agent-action]]. Ambiguous phrasing needs clarifying questions.
