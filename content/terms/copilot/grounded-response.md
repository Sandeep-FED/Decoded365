## Beginner
A grounded response is an answer that was based on real sources, not just the AI's memory.

## Maker
It is more likely to be accurate and specific, and often comes with [[citation|citations]].

## Architect
It is the result of [[grounding]]. It lowers, but does not eliminate, the risk of [[hallucination]].
