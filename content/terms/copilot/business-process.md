## Beginner
A business process is a repeatable set of steps that gets a job done, like approving an expense.

## Maker
Copilot, agents, [[power-automate]] or [[copilot-studio]] can help with or automate parts of it. People usually still handle the judgement calls.

## Architect
Map the process first, then decide which steps are retrieval, which are decisions and which are actions. Each automated step needs an owner, permissions and a way to handle exceptions.
