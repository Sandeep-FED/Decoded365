## Beginner
Finance Agents are AI assistants made for finance teams, to help with finance tasks.

## Maker
They were called Copilot for Finance until April 2025. Like any [[agent]] they work within the access of the person using them. Check the current list of agents and what each one can do, because the line-up changes.

## Architect
Access to finance data must follow your existing permissions, and any step that changes financial records needs the approvals you would require of a person. Validate outputs against source records before relying on them.
