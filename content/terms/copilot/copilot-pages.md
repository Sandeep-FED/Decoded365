## Beginner
Copilot Pages are shared pages where you keep the good bits of an AI chat and keep working on them with others.

## Maker
A Page is a shared, editable canvas. Turn any Copilot response into a living document that your team can co-edit with Copilot still in the loop, instead of losing a useful answer in a long conversation.

## Architect
Pages build on the same collaboration technology as [[loop-component|Loop components]], so content stays editable and can be shared. Sharing follows normal permissions.
