## Beginner
A background task is work an agent keeps doing while you get on with something else.

## Maker
You start the job and don't have to stay in the conversation. When it finishes, or needs you, you can get a [[notification]].

## Architect
Long-running work should be resumable and observable, with [[trace|traces]] or [[log|logs]], and with limits on what the agent can do while nobody is watching.
