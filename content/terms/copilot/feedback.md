## Beginner
Feedback is you telling the AI whether an answer was good or bad.

## Maker
A thumbs up or down, or a comment, helps builders and admins see where answers fall short and improve instructions.

## Architect
Treat feedback as input to [[evaluation]]. Be aware that comments can contain sensitive information, so know where they are stored and who can read them.
