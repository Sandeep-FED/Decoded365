## Beginner
An information barrier is a wall between two groups of people that stops them communicating or sharing.

## Maker
Organisations use it to meet regulatory or conflict-of-interest rules, for example between departments.

## Architect
It is a [[microsoft-purview]] control. Consider how barriers interact with Copilot's retrieval and with shared content.
