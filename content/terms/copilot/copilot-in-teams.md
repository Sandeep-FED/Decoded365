## Beginner
Copilot in Teams is an AI helper for meetings and chats that can summarise what was said and list actions.

## Maker
It helps you catch up on a conversation, find decisions and follow-ups, and produce a [[meeting-recap]].

## Architect
Meeting features depend on transcripts or recordings being available and allowed by policy. See [[transcript]] and [[retention]] for how that content is handled.
