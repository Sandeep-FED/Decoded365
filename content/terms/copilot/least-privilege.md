## Beginner
Least privilege means giving a person or app only the rights needed to do its job, and nothing extra.

## Maker
Narrow rights limit what can go wrong. This matters more for agents that can act on your behalf.

## Architect
Apply it to users, apps and agent identities. Review permissions regularly and combine it with [[role-based-access-control]].
