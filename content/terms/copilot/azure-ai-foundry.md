## Beginner
Azure AI Foundry is Microsoft's workshop in the cloud for building, testing and running AI applications and agents.

## Maker
Developers use it to choose models, connect data and tools, evaluate results and run what they build with enterprise controls. It is more developer-focused than low-code tools such as [[copilot-studio]].

## Architect
It brings together models, tooling, [[evaluation]] and operations on Azure. Microsoft renames and reorganises its AI services fairly often, so check the current product name and features before planning.
