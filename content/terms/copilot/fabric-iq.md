## Beginner
Fabric IQ is a Microsoft layer that helps AI make sense of an organisation's analytical data.

## Maker
It is associated with Microsoft Fabric and helps agents work with governed data and analytical context, rather than raw tables.

## Architect
It is one of the "IQ" layers alongside [[work-iq]]. Details are evolving, so confirm what it provides in current documentation.
