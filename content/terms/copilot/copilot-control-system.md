## Beginner
The Copilot Control System is the collection of admin tools for keeping Copilot and agents safe, managed and measured.

## Maker
It gathers security, management and measurement capabilities so administrators can govern how Copilot and agents are used in the organisation.

## Architect
It is an umbrella for controls across data protection, management, and reporting, including things such as the [[copilot-dashboard]]. Check the current list of included capabilities, as it evolves.
