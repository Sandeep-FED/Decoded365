## Beginner
Isolation keeps things apart so a problem or a breach in one place can't spread to others.

## Maker
Agents, environments and data can be separated so that an agent can't reach anything outside what it is meant to.

## Architect
Use separate [[environment|environments]], identities and permissions. It is a core part of limiting blast radius.
