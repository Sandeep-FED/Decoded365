## Beginner
A generative answer is a reply the AI composes on the spot, rather than picking a pre-written one.

## Maker
In an agent, it lets the agent answer from its knowledge sources even where you haven't scripted a topic for that question.

## Architect
It is produced by a model using retrieved [[knowledge-source|knowledge]]. Fixed [[topic|topics]] give predictability; generative answers give breadth. Many agents combine both.
