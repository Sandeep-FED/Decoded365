## Beginner
Enterprise data protection is the set of safeguards that keeps your prompts and answers within your organisation's protections.

## Maker
It applies to eligible work AI experiences and helps ensure the organisation's information is handled under its enterprise terms and controls.

## Architect
The protections apply to specific products and configurations, so verify coverage per feature. Related earlier names appear under [[microsoft-365-copilot-chat]].
