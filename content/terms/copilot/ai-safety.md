## Beginner
AI safety is the set of habits and controls that stop an AI from causing harm or doing something unexpected.

## Maker
It covers harmful or misleading output, security attacks and unintended actions. Admins and builders use content filters, [[guardrail|guardrails]], testing and review to reduce those risks.

## Architect
Safety work spans design, testing and operation: for example [[red-teaming]], [[moderation]], [[prompt-injection]] defences and monitoring in production. It overlaps with [[responsible-ai]], which is the broader set of principles.
