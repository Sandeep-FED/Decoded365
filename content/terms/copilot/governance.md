## Beginner
Governance is the set of rules and responsibilities that decide how Copilot and agents may be created and used.

## Maker
It covers who can build agents, what data they can reach, how they are monitored and what happens when something goes wrong.

## Architect
Combine [[policy|policies]], [[lifecycle-management]], monitoring and ownership. Start early, because agents can spread quickly once users can create them.
