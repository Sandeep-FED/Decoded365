## Beginner
A tenant is your organisation's own private space in Microsoft's cloud.

## Maker
It holds your users, licences, policies, apps and data, separate from other organisations.

## Architect
Settings such as policies and [[data-boundary|data boundaries]] are tenant-level, while an [[environment]] is a narrower container inside it.
