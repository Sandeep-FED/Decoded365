## Beginner
Copilot in Excel is an AI helper inside spreadsheets that can explain numbers and write formulas for you.

## Maker
You can ask it to analyse data, spot trends, build formulas or create summaries, which is useful when you are not sure which function to use.

## Architect
It works on the data in the workbook and, where permitted, other work content. Check its output, since formulas and calculations from AI still need [[verification]]. Tidy tables help it considerably.
