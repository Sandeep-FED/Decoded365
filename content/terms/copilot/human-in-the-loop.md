## Beginner
Human-in-the-loop means a person checks or approves what the AI does before it counts.

## Maker
This is sensible for important outputs and actions such as sending emails, changing records or spending money.

## Architect
Put approvals where risk is high and make the review easy and informed. Rubber-stamping without context defeats the purpose.
