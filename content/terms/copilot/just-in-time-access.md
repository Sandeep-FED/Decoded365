## Beginner
Just-in-time access gives someone powerful rights only when they need them, and only for a short while.

## Maker
It stops people holding standing admin rights that could be misused or stolen.

## Architect
It is a form of privileged access management. Combine it with approval, time limits and logging. It supports [[least-privilege]].
