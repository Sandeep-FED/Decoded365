## Beginner
Red teaming is deliberately trying to break an AI system to find its weak spots.

## Maker
Testers use tricky, hostile or unusual prompts so problems are found before real users find them.

## Architect
It targets safety and security weaknesses such as [[jailbreak|jailbreaks]] and [[prompt-injection]], and complements ordinary [[evaluation]].
