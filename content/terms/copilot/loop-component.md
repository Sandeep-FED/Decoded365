## Beginner
A Loop component is a small live block of content, such as a list or table, that stays in sync wherever it is shared.

## Maker
Edit it in a chat and the same change appears in an email or document. It works well with AI-generated working content.

## Architect
Components stay synchronised across apps and follow sharing permissions of where they are stored.
