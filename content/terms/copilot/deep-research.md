## Beginner
Deep research is when an AI spends longer digging through many sources and then writes a thorough report, instead of giving a quick reply.

## Maker
It is slower than an ordinary chat answer but can give more developed results with sources. Use it for questions that need real investigation.

## Architect
It typically plans queries, retrieves from many sources, reasons over findings and assembles a report with references. Check the sources it cites, as quality depends on what it could reach.
