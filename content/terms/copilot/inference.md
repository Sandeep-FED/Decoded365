## Beginner
Inference is the moment a trained AI actually answers a new request.

## Maker
Training builds the model once. Inference is the day-to-day work of using it.

## Architect
Inference cost and [[latency]] depend on model size and input length. Capacity limits and [[quota|quotas]] affect it.
