## Beginner
Agent 365 is a control panel for the AI agents an organisation uses, so someone can see them and keep them in check.

## Maker
It is described as a management and governance layer for discovering, securing, controlling and observing agents across an organisation. It matters once many people start creating [[agent|agents]] and the organisation needs an overview.

## Architect
Treat it as the governance side of agents: inventory, access, security and monitoring. It sits alongside the wider [[copilot-control-system]], and its detailed capabilities can change, so confirm current behaviour in Microsoft's own documentation.
