## Beginner
A variable is a named box that holds a value for later, such as what the user typed.

## Maker
Agents and workflows use them to remember inputs and results from earlier steps.

## Architect
Scope and lifetime matter: some are limited to a topic and some persist. Avoid storing secrets in them.
