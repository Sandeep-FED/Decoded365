## Beginner
An OpenAI model is an AI model made by the company OpenAI.

## Maker
Microsoft uses OpenAI technology in several Copilot and Azure AI experiences, alongside its own services and controls.

## Architect
The model is only one layer of the product. Data handling, grounding and safety are provided by Microsoft's services around it.
