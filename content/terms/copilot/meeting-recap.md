## Beginner
A meeting recap is a summary of a meeting: key points, decisions and who is doing what.

## Maker
It saves you from re-reading everything or watching the recording. It can also summarise a chat or document.

## Architect
Where available it relies on a [[transcript]] or recording, so it needs to be enabled and permitted.
