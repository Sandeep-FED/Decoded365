## Beginner
The Microsoft 365 Copilot app is the home screen for Microsoft 365 on the web and your devices, where you start work, find your files and open Copilot.

## Maker
It was called the Microsoft 365 app until December 2024. It brings your recent files, apps and Copilot Chat into one place. Do not confuse it with [[microsoft-365-copilot]], which is the AI assistant rather than the app that hosts it.

## Architect
It is the front door to Microsoft 365 experiences and one of the places where people meet [[agent|agents]] next to chat. Administrators decide which apps and agents appear, so treat it as part of rollout planning rather than as a separate product.
