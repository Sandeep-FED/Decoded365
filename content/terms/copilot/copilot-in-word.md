## Beginner
Copilot in Word is an AI writing helper that can draft, rewrite and summarise documents for you.

## Maker
You can ask it to write a first version, change the tone, compare drafts or turn notes into a structured document.

## Architect
It can draw on other documents you point it to. Treat its output as a [[draft]]: check facts, names and anything that could be a [[hallucination]].
