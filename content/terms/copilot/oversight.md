## Beginner
Oversight is people keeping an eye on AI systems so they stay useful, safe and on track.

## Maker
Organisations assign owners and review how AI is used and what it produces.

## Architect
Oversight spans accountability, monitoring and escalation. It is broader than approving individual outputs, which is [[human-in-the-loop]].
