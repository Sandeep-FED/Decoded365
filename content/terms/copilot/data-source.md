## Beginner
A data source is any place an AI helper can look things up, such as SharePoint, email or a database.

## Maker
Admins and builders decide which data sources are available. The more sources, the more the AI can answer, and the more care you need over access.

## Architect
Sources may be Microsoft 365 services, websites or external systems reached by a [[connector]] or [[graph-connector]]. In an agent, the chosen ones are configured as [[knowledge-source|knowledge sources]].
