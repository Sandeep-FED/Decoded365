## Beginner
Microsoft splits Copilot into two kinds of work: everyday AI, which your licence already pays for, and advanced AI, which you pay for as you use it.

## Maker
Everyday AI is covered by the user licence. Advanced AI means long-running agentic work, such as [[cowork]], Code, [[autopilot]] and frontier models, and it runs on usage-based billing in [[copilot-credits]]. Knowing which side a feature falls on explains why a licensed user can still see extra charges.

## Architect
Plan budgets by workload, not only by seat: licences cover the everyday side, while credit consumption scales with how much advanced work is run. Monitor consumption and set limits, because the boundary between the two can change as products evolve.
