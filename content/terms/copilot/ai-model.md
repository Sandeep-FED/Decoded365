## Beginner
An AI model is the trained "brain" inside an AI tool: it has learned patterns from lots of examples and uses them to produce an answer.

## Maker
Different models are good at different things, such as writing, images or numbers. Products like [[microsoft-365-copilot]] wrap a model with extra services for search, security and the apps you use.

## Architect
A model transforms an input into a predicted or generated output. In Copilot the model is only one part: [[orchestration]], [[grounding]], permissions and safety controls surround it, so the same model can behave differently in different products.
