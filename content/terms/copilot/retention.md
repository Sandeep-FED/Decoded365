## Beginner
Retention is the rule for how long content is kept before it is deleted or reviewed.

## Maker
It applies to documents, records, chats and audit information, and may apply to AI conversations too.

## Architect
Align retention settings with legal and business needs, as they affect what can be found and recovered.
