## Beginner
Identity is the verified "who" behind an action, whether a person, a service or an agent.

## Maker
Access, audit and permitted actions all depend on identity. If Copilot acts for you, it acts with your identity and rights.

## Architect
Agents may run under the user's identity or their own. Choose deliberately, since it decides what the agent can reach and how actions are audited.
