## Beginner
The standard harness is the engine for agents that follow the conversation paths and rules that you write, so they behave predictably.

## Maker
It runs the [[topic|topics]] and rules you author, which makes conversations structured and repeatable. Its usage is covered for employees who have a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence and use it under their own identity. Unlicensed users, autonomous runs and computer use are still billed.

## Architect
Check the cost for each case rather than assuming the licence covers everything: coverage depends on whose identity is used and whether the run is autonomous. See the [[microsoft-copilot-harness]] for the fully usage-billed alternative.
