## Beginner
Generation is the AI writing its answer, one small piece at a time.

## Maker
You see the answer appear gradually because the model produces it piece by piece based on your request and the context.

## Architect
The model predicts the next [[token]] repeatedly until it stops. It is the output part of [[inference]].
