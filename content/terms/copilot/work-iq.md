## Beginner
Work IQ is Microsoft's way of giving Copilot and agents an understanding of how your work happens.

## Maker
It brings together data, memory and inference so agents can use real organisational context.

## Architect
It is described in layers: [[work-iq-data-layer]], [[work-iq-memory-layer]] and [[work-iq-inference-layer]], with access through [[work-iq-api]] and [[work-iq-mcp]]. Details are evolving; verify in current documentation.
