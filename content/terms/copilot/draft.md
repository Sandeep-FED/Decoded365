## Beginner
A draft is a first version written by AI that you are meant to read and improve.

## Maker
Think of it as a head start. You check it, fix it and make it yours before it goes out.

## Architect
Calling output a draft is a [[human-in-the-loop]] control: a person remains accountable for what is sent, signed or published.
