## Beginner
Copilot in Outlook is an AI helper in your email that can draft replies and summarise long threads.

## Maker
It helps write, tidy and shorten messages and catch up on busy conversations, and where supported it can help with calendar tasks.

## Architect
It uses the mailbox and other content the user can access. Review drafts before sending, and keep sensitive-information rules in mind, as with any [[draft]] written by AI.
