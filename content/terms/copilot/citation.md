## Beginner
A citation is a pointer in an answer that shows where a statement came from, like a footnote.

## Maker
Citations let you open the document or page behind a claim and check it. They make AI answers easier to trust and easier to correct.

## Architect
They are only as good as the retrieval behind them, so a citation shows a source was used, not that the statement is correct. Use them as part of [[verification]] and to reduce [[hallucination]] risk.
