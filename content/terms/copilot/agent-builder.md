## Beginner
Agent Builder is a quick way for people to create a simple agent for themselves or their team, mostly by describing what it should do.

## Maker
It is aimed at everyday users rather than specialists. It was renamed Copilot Studio Lite in September 2025 and went back to Agent Builder in November 2025, so you will see both names. For more control, such as connecting other systems and managing agents at scale, use [[copilot-studio]].

## Architect
Treat agents made this way as lightweight and give each one a clear owner. Decide who may create and share them, what knowledge they may use, and when a fuller build in [[copilot-studio]] is justified. Check current capabilities, because the product has changed name and scope more than once.
