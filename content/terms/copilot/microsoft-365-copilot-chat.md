## Beginner
Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic) is the chat assistant for work: you type a question and get an answer, with your organisation's data protections in place.

## Maker
It began as Bing Chat Enterprise and went through several names, including Microsoft Copilot with Enterprise Data Protection and Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, before becoming Basic in April 2026. It is a chat experience. The fuller [[microsoft-365-copilot|Premium]] experience adds Copilot inside Microsoft 365 apps and deeper use of your work data, so check current plans for the exact split.

## Architect
Because the name changed so often, logs and older admin articles may use any of the earlier names for the same experience. Data protection applies to prompts and responses, and administrators can control access and which [[agent|agents]] are available. Confirm current capabilities and licensing for each plan in your own tenant.
