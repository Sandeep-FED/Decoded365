## Beginner
Non-deterministic output means asking the same question twice can give two different, equally reasonable answers.

## Maker
Don't expect identical wording each time. For checks, look at whether the substance is right.

## Architect
Randomness is controlled by settings such as [[temperature]], where exposed. Evaluate on many runs rather than one.
