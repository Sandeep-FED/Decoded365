## Beginner
A key phrase is a short phrase that captures an important idea in some content.

## Maker
Pulling out key phrases helps with search, sorting and summaries.

## Architect
Key phrase extraction is a language technique, related to [[named-entity-recognition]], and is useful as a signal for classification and search.
