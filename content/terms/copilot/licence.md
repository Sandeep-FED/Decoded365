## Beginner
A licence is what you pay for or are assigned that unlocks a feature for you.

## Maker
Which Copilot features, agents and admin tools you get depends on your licence, and some use is billed by consumption instead.

## Architect
Licensing and usage allowances change often, so check current Microsoft documentation. See also [[usage-based-billing]].
