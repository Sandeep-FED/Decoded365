## Beginner
Usage-based billing means you pay for what you use rather than a flat fee.

## Maker
For some agents and Copilot features, charges depend on how much they are used. In Copilot this is measured in [[copilot-credits]], and it covers the advanced, long-running work described in [[everyday-ai-and-advanced-ai]], such as [[cowork]] and [[autopilot]], while the user licence covers everyday use.

## Architect
Estimate volume, set budgets and monitor consumption. Coverage differs by workload and engine: for example the [[microsoft-copilot-harness]] bills credits for all usage, while the [[standard-harness]] is covered for licensed employees using it under their own identity. Pricing changes, so check current documentation.
