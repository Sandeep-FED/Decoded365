## Beginner
Bring your own data means plugging your organisation's own information into an AI tool so its answers fit your business.

## Maker
You choose which approved sources the experience may use, such as files, sites or a database, rather than relying on general knowledge alone.

## Architect
Choose sources deliberately: each one widens what the AI can disclose. See [[knowledge-source]] and [[permission-trimming]] for how access is kept consistent with existing rights.
