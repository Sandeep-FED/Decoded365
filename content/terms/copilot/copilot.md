## Beginner
Copilot is Microsoft's name for the AI helpers built into its products, from Word and Teams to Windows and Azure.

## Maker
The name is used broadly, so check which Copilot someone means. [[microsoft-365-copilot]] works with work data in Microsoft 365 apps, while other Copilots have other scopes.

## Architect
Treat "Copilot" as a brand family, not a single product. Capabilities, licensing and data handling differ per product, so always qualify the name.
