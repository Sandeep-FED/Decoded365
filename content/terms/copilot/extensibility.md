## Beginner
Extensibility is the ability to add new abilities to Copilot, like adding attachments to a toolbox.

## Maker
You can extend Copilot with agents, data sources, connectors, actions and your own business logic.

## Architect
Options include [[declarative-agent|declarative agents]], [[custom-engine-agent|custom engine agents]], APIs and [[model-context-protocol|MCP]] servers. Choose based on how much control you need over orchestration and hosting.
