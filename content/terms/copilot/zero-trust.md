## Beginner
Zero Trust means never assuming anyone is safe just because they are on the company network.

## Maker
Every request is checked, rights are kept small and you plan as if a breach has happened.

## Architect
Built on explicit verification, [[least-privilege]] and assume-breach. It applies equally to agents.
