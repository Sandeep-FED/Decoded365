## Beginner
General availability, or GA, means a feature is officially ready for everyone to use.

## Maker
It is the release stage after [[preview]]. GA features are covered by Microsoft's published service terms for broad production use.

## Architect
Check each feature's stage before depending on it. Support, SLAs and change notice differ from [[preview]] features.
