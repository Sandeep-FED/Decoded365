## Beginner
Job automation is using AI and tools to take over the repeatable parts of someone's work.

## Maker
The routine steps are handled automatically, while people keep the judgement calls and the accountability.

## Architect
Decide which tasks are rule-based enough to automate and which need a person. Keep an owner for exceptions and a way to audit what ran.
