## Beginner
Analyst is a ready-made Copilot helper that is good at working with numbers and data.

## Maker
It is a Microsoft 365 Copilot agent aimed at data analysis, calculations and structured reasoning, turning business information into insights. Pair it with [[researcher]], which is aimed at wide research across sources.

## Architect
Like other built-in agents, what it can read depends on the signed-in user's permissions and on how the organisation has enabled it. Availability and licensing can change, so check current documentation.
