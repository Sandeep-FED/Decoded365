## Beginner
Cowork is a Copilot mode where you describe a whole job and the AI plans it, does the steps across your tools and hands back a finished result.

## Maker
It is Copilot's agentic system for delegated work. You describe a multi-step task and Cowork plans the steps, runs them across your tools and returns the finished result. It can run on demand, on a schedule, or when an email or Teams message arrives. It has been generally available since 16 June 2026, and needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence plus usage-based [[copilot-credits]].

## Architect
Cowork sits on the advanced, usage-billed side of [[everyday-ai-and-advanced-ai]], so cost follows how much work it runs, not just how many licensed users you have. As an example of [[agentic-ai]] it acts across tools, so review the actions it takes, the scope of access it has and the triggers you set up. Capabilities and availability may change; confirm in current documentation.
