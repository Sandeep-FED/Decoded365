## Beginner
Business data is the information a company holds about its work: documents, emails, meetings, reports and records.

## Maker
It gives AI answers something real to be based on. Without it the AI can only fall back on general knowledge.

## Architect
It is the raw material for [[grounding]]. Access follows existing permissions, so poor permissions or labels on business data can lead to oversharing through AI.
