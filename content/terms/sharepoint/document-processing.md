## Beginner
Document Processing for Microsoft 365 is a group of AI features that help read, sort and pull information out of documents stored in places such as SharePoint, so people do less manual filing.

## Maker
Typical uses are classifying documents, extracting details such as dates and amounts into columns of a [[document-library]], and starting a follow-up process. It has been known as Microsoft Syntex and as Microsoft Syntex & SharePoint Premium, so older articles use those names. Check which features need extra licences before you plan around them.

## Architect
The features act on content in SharePoint libraries and write what they find into columns, which other tools can then search, filter and automate on. Treat accuracy as something to measure on your own documents, and confirm per-feature availability and billing for your tenant.
