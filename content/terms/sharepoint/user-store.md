## Beginner
A user store is a list of people, groups and their rights that belongs to another security system.

## Maker
It is a logical grouping of users, groups and content permissions for a third-party security or content system that FAST Search Authorization accesses.

## Architect
The store belongs to a third-party system. See [[default-user-store]] and [[principal-aliasing]].
