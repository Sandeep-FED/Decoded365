## Beginner
Open web permission is the right to open a site, needed when you export or import it.

## Maker
It is a requisite permission during the import or export of a SharePoint site. Without it the operation cannot open the site.

## Architect
The permission is required for import and export. It differs from [[open-item-permission]], which concerns a single file.
