## Beginner
An extracted term is a word or phrase that search has picked out and has an explanation for.

## Maker
It is a term that an [[extracted-definition]] applies to. Search can then show the definition next to the term.

## Architect
The term is paired with the [[extracted-definition]] found during the crawl.
