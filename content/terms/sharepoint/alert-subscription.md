## Beginner
An alert subscription is a request that says: tell me by message whenever this thing changes.

## Maker
You ask to be told when something you care about changes, such as a document, web page, list item, site or other resource. The messages are sent for you automatically once your chosen conditions are met.

## Architect
A subscription is a request to receive an Internet message when user-defined criteria are met. The messages are generated automatically when matching items change, so the criteria you define are what keep the volume manageable.
