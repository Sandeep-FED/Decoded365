## Beginner
A published version is the approved copy that everyone can see.

## Maker
It is the version of a list item that is approved and can be seen by all users. The user interface version number is incremented to the next major version, and the minor version is zero. See [[version-history]].

## Architect
The number moves to the next positive major version and the minor part is zero. The [[publishing-level]] indicates the publishing status of a version.
