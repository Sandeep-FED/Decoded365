## Beginner
Open Item permission is the right to open and read a whole file.

## Maker
It is an authorisation that allows a user to retrieve an entire file. Compare it with [[open-web-permission]].

## Architect
The permission covers retrieval of an entire file. Permission levels, such as those of [[sharepoint-groups]], build on rights like this.
