## Beginner
A workflow task is one job in the chain of a business process.

## Maker
It is an action or task in a sequence that is related to a built-in or user-defined business process. Tasks are kept in the [[workflow-task-list]].

## Architect
The task is part of the sequence and is stored in the [[workflow-task-list]].
