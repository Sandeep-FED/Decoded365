## Beginner
A contextual search scope narrows a search to the place you are in, such as one site or one list.

## Maker
It is a system-defined restriction that can be added to a query so that results come only from a specific site or list. People get results relevant to where they are searching from.

## Architect
The scope is system-defined and optional. When it is added to a query it restricts results to items from a particular site or list.
