## Beginner
AppSource is Microsoft's online marketplace for ready-made business apps and add-ons, including ones for SharePoint, from Microsoft and other companies.

## Maker
Instead of building something yourself or asking developers to add it to your own [[app-catalog]], you can find and add apps from the marketplace. Administrators decide whether people may get apps this way. It was previously known as the Office Store.

## Architect
Partners list offers in the commercial marketplace. Solutions built with the [[spfx]] can be offered through it. Review each listing's permissions and data handling before allowing it in your tenant, in the same way as for a solution you deploy yourself.
