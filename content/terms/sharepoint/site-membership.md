## Beginner
Site membership means you belong to a site and have been given a set of rights on it.

## Maker
It is the status of being a member of a site and having a defined set of user rights to access or manage the content. Groups such as [[members-group]] give the rights.

## Architect
Rights are granted through groups. See [[sharepoint-groups]].
