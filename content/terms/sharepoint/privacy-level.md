## Beginner
The privacy level decides who is allowed to see your personal details on your profile.

## Maker
It is a setting that specifies the category of users who are allowed to view the personal information of other users, such as user profile properties, colleagues or memberships.

## Architect
The setting controls who sees data of the [[user-profile-store]], so check the level when profile data seems to be hidden.
