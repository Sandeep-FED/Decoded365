## Beginner
The Visitors group is the standard group for people who may look but not change things.

## Maker
It is a default group of users on a SharePoint site. By default the Visitors group is assigned the Read permission level. See [[sharepoint-groups]].

## Architect
The default permission level is Read. The [[members-group]] has Contribute by default.
