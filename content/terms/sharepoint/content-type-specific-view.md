## Beginner
A content type specific view is a special way of showing a list for just one kind of item.

## Maker
It is a view associated with a particular content type that is attached to a folder. People who open that folder see the view designed for that content type.

## Architect
The view is tied to a [[content-type]] that is associated with a [[list-folder|folder]]. It is useful when different folders should show their items differently.
