## Beginner
A workflow history item is one entry in the story of what a workflow has done.

## Maker
It is a list item that stores information about the current status of, and past actions for, a document or item associated with a workflow. It is stored in the [[workflow-history-list]].

## Architect
Each item belongs to the [[workflow-history-list]].
