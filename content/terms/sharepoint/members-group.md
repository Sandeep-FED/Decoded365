## Beginner
The Members group is the standard group for the people who do the day-to-day work on a site.

## Maker
It is a default group of users on a SharePoint site. By default the Members group is assigned the Contribute permission level. See [[sharepoint-groups]].

## Architect
The default permission level is Contribute. Compare with the [[visitors-group]], which is given Read by default.
