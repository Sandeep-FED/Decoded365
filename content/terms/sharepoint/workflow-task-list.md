## Beginner
The workflow task list is the to-do list of a business process.

## Maker
It is a list that stores the sequence of actions or tasks for a business process. It differs from the [[workflow-history-list]].

## Architect
The list holds [[workflow-task|workflow tasks]] in sequence.
