## Beginner
A navigation structure is the signposting of a site: the links that lead you between its parts.

## Maker
It is a hierarchical organisation of links between related content, such as lists within a site. It is what menus and quick-launch links are built from.

## Architect
The structure is hierarchical, so links can be grouped under parents. Design it around how people look for things, not around how the site is built.
