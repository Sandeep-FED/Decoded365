## Beginner
Static rank is how important search thinks an item is, independent of what you typed.

## Maker
It is the component of a rank that does not depend on the search query. It represents the perceived importance of an item and can relate to its origin, to the relationships between the item and other items, or to business rules defined in the search application.

## Architect
It does not change with the query, unlike [[dynamic-rank]]. Business rules from the search application can feed into it.
