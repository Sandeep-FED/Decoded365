## Beginner
A list form is the page where you fill in or look at the details of one list item.

## Maker
It is a page that lets users create, view or edit an item in a [[sharepoint-list]]. The page that creates a new item is the [[new-form]].

## Architect
The form is shown through the [[list-form-web-part]]. Different forms exist for creating, viewing and editing.
