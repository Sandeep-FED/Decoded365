## Beginner
This is the search engine service of the older Office SharePoint Server product.

## Maker
It is the farm-wide service that either responds to query requests from front-end web servers or crawls items. It belongs to older on-premises SharePoint.

## Architect
The service takes one of two roles: answering queries from front-end web servers, or crawling items. It is a [[sharepoint-server]] feature.
