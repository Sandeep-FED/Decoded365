## Beginner
The default list view is the way a list looks when you open it without choosing anything special.

## Maker
It is the view of a [[sharepoint-list]] that the list owner defines to appear when people browse to the list without specifying a view. Pick it with care, because most visitors only ever see that view.

## Architect
The owner of the list defines which view acts as the default. Mobile browsing has its own equivalent, the [[default-mobile-list-view]].
