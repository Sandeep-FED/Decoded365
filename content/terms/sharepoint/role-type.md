## Beginner
A role type is one of the standard permission sets that come ready-made.

## Maker
It is a predefined role definition. See [[role-identifier]] for the number that identifies a role within a site.

## Architect
Predefined role definitions are the basis for permission levels, which are assigned to [[sharepoint-groups]].
