## Beginner
The workflow history list is the logbook of a business process.

## Maker
It is a list that stores the history of actions or tasks for a business process. It is made up of [[workflow-history-item|history items]].

## Architect
It differs from the [[workflow-task-list]], which stores the sequence of tasks.
