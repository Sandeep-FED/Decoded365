## Beginner
A descendant content type is a child that inherits from a parent, the way a family recipe is adapted for a new dish.

## Maker
It is any [[content-type]] that inherits from another content type. Changes to the parent can flow down to the descendants.

## Architect
Inheritance means that a descendant starts with the settings of its parent. Plan the hierarchy carefully, since many content types can descend from the same one.
