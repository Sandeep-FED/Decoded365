## Beginner
Business Connectivity Services lets you bring data from other business systems into SharePoint and Office, so people can use it without leaving those tools.

## Maker
The feature lets users work with back-end line-of-business (LOB) data from inside the Office suite and SharePoint. It is known by several names, including BCS, Business Connectivity and Microsoft Business Connectivity Services.

## Architect
BCS connects SharePoint and the Office suite to back-end data. A solution can be deployed to clients; see [[bcs-solution-deployment]]. Search for any of the variant names to land on this one concept.
