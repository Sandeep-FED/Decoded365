## Beginner
A content type group is a labelled shelf where similar content types are kept together.

## Maker
It is a named category used to organise [[content-type|content types]] that have a similar purpose. It makes a long list of content types easier to browse.

## Architect
The group is only an organising name. It does not change how an individual [[content-type]] behaves.
