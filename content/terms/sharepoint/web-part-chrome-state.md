## Beginner
The chrome state says whether a web part is shown open or collapsed.

## Maker
It is the condition of a [[web-part]] and the chrome around it. The possible values are zero (0) for normal state and one (1) for minimised state.

## Architect
Zero means normal and one means minimised.
