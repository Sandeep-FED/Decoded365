## Beginner
The List Form web part is the building block that draws a list form on a page.

## Maker
It is a [[web-part]] used to display, edit or view an item in a list. It is what you see on a [[list-form]].

## Architect
The part renders the item for display, editing or viewing inside a [[list-form]].
