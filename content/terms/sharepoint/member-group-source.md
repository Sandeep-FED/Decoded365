## Beginner
The member group source says where a group of people comes from, such as a particular company directory.

## Maker
It is a qualified domain name, such as domain.corp.microsoft.com, that identifies the source of a [[member-group]].

## Architect
The value is a qualified domain name. It names the source of the [[member-group]].
