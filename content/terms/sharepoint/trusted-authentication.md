## Beginner
Trusted authentication lets one account act on behalf of the person who is currently signed in.

## Maker
It is a mechanism whereby a user account or a process account can be used to perform operations on behalf of the current user.

## Architect
An account is trusted to perform operations for the current user. It is different from a [[trusted-subsystem]], which is a two-way trust between server components.
