## Beginner
A site collection administrator is the person who is in charge of a whole group of sites.

## Maker
It is a user who has administrative permissions for a [[site-collection]]. It is narrower than the farm-wide role of the [[farm-administrators-group]].

## Architect
The role covers one site collection only. Farm-level rights belong to the [[farm-administrators-group]].
