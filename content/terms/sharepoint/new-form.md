## Beginner
A new form is the page you fill in to add a new item to a list.

## Maker
It is a form that allows for the creation of a list item. It is one of the [[list-form|list forms]].

## Architect
It is the creation counterpart of the view and edit forms; all of them are [[list-form|list forms]].
