## Beginner
An authoritative page is a web page an administrator has marked as especially trustworthy, so search shows it nearer the top.

## Maker
A site collection administrator picks pages that are more relevant than others, typically the home page of the organisation's intranet. The higher the page's authority, the higher it tends to appear in search results.

## Architect
Authority is expressed as an [[authority-level]], and the page is identified by its URL. Both names, authoritative page and authority page, refer to the same concept.
