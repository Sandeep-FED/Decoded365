## Beginner
The search catalog is the big collection of facts that search gathered, used to answer your questions.

## Maker
It is all of the crawl data that is associated with a given [[search-application]]. It provides the information used to generate the query results.

## Architect
The catalog is what queries read from. Its data is generated by crawling.
