## Beginner
A feature definition is the instruction sheet for a feature, saying what it is called and what it can do.

## Maker
It is an XML fragment that defines a feature and its attributes. The attributes include things such as its identifier and where it can be activated.

## Architect
The definition is a fragment of XML. Its attributes identify the feature (see [[feature-identifier]]) and state its [[feature-scope]].
