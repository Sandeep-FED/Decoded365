## Beginner
A crawled property is a piece of information the search robot finds about an item, like the author name on a book cover.

## Maker
It is a type of metadata that can be discovered during a crawl and applied to one or more items. You can map it to a managed property to make it searchable in a controlled way.

## Architect
Crawled properties are discovered during a crawl and can be applied to several items. They become useful for queries and refiners once mapped to a managed property. See also [[metadata-index]].
