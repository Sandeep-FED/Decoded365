## Beginner
An activity feed is like a news ticker of what the people, documents and topics you follow have been up to.

## Maker
It collects notifications and updates about people, documents and tags that you follow, so you can catch up in one place instead of visiting each item. It is one of the social features of SharePoint.

## Architect
The feed is built from user-profile and social data, so what appears depends on what a person follows. Treat it as a presentation of changes rather than as an audit log, because it only reflects the things followed.
