## Beginner
The feature scope says how wide a feature reaches when it is turned on: one site, a whole group of sites, and so on.

## Maker
It is the scope at which a feature can be activated. Choosing the scope decides where the feature's effect applies.

## Architect
The scope is defined as part of the [[feature-definition]]. Pick the narrowest scope that does the job.
