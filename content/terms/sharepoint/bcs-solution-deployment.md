## Beginner
BCS solution deployment is the process of getting a business-data solution from the server onto people's computers.

## Maker
It is a way of deploying a server-side [[business-connectivity-services]] solution to client computers. It is based on ClickOnce technology, so the solution installs on the client.

## Architect
The deployment is a server-to-client scenario that is based on ClickOnce. Plan for client machines to receive the solution through that mechanism.
