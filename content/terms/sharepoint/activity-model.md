## Beginner
An activity model is a ready-made routine, like a saved checklist of steps that you can reuse.

## Maker
It is a predefined sequence of actions. Instead of rebuilding the same steps each time, you reuse the model inside a bigger [[activity-flow]].

## Architect
Models are building blocks: an [[activity-flow]] can mix single actions and activity model instances in any order. Reuse them to keep workflows consistent and to avoid copying the same sequence into several places.
