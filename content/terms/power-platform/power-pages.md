## Beginner
Power Pages is for building websites that people outside your organisation can use, such as a customer portal or a sign-up page, without writing a website from scratch.

## Maker
You design a site in a visual studio from templates and connect it to the same business data as your apps in [[dataverse]]. It was previously called Power Apps portals. Compare it with a [[communication-site]] in SharePoint, which shares information inside the organisation.

## Architect
Power Pages is a low-code software-as-a-service platform for external-facing business websites. Site data is stored and secured in [[dataverse]], where web roles and table permissions control what visitors can read or change. Developers can extend sites with Liquid templates and code, and sites move between environments with [[solution|solutions]].
