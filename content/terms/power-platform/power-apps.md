## Beginner
Power Apps lets people build business apps without being professional programmers, by placing things on a screen and describing behaviour with simple formulas.

## Maker
There are two main kinds of app. A [[canvas-app]] gives you full control over the layout, and a [[model-driven-app]] is generated from your data in [[dataverse]]. Apps can be shared with colleagues and run in a browser or on a phone.

## Architect
Apps reach data through [[connector|connectors]] or, for model-driven apps, natively through [[dataverse]]. Logic is written in [[power-fx]]. Package apps in a [[solution]] to move them between [[environment|environments]]. People need access to the app and to every data source it uses.
