## Beginner
The Power Platform admin center is the control room where administrators look after environments, settings and policies.

## Maker
It is a unified portal for managing [[environment|environments]] and settings for Power Apps, Power Automate and Dynamics 365 apps. This is where you create environments, set [[data-policy|data policies]] and check usage. It is separate from the Microsoft 365 admin center.

## Architect
Administrative actions can also be scripted through the Power Platform CLI, PowerShell and APIs, which is how larger organisations automate environment creation and governance. Administration of [[power-bi]] has its own separate settings.
