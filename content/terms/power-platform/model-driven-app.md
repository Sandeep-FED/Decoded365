## Beginner
A model-driven app is built around your data: you decide what information you keep and the app builds the screens for it. It is like starting from a filing system rather than a blank page.

## Maker
You choose tables, forms, views and dashboards and describe how people move between them in a [[model-driven-site-map|site map]]. It needs [[dataverse]], and it adapts to phone, tablet and desktop. Choose it when the data and its rules matter more than a custom look; choose a [[canvas-app]] for a bespoke layout.

## Architect
An app is a set of components, such as tables, forms, views, dashboards and pages, defined in the app designer and delivered through the Unified Interface. Behaviour comes from the data model: [[dataverse-column|columns]], relationships, business rules, [[business-process-flow|business process flows]] and [[security-role|security roles]]. Ship it in a [[solution]].
