## Beginner
Power BI turns data into charts and reports so people can see what is happening in the business at a glance.

## Maker
Power BI connects to many data sources, and [[dataverse]] is only one of them. Reports can be embedded in a [[model-driven-app]] or used on their own. Its reports are not stored in Dataverse, and its administration is separate from the Power Platform admin center.

## Architect
Power BI is made of semantic models, reports and dashboards published to workspaces. Models use import or live connections, and refresh schedules and row-level security are configured in the Power BI service rather than in [[dataverse]].
