## Beginner
The site map is the menu of a model-driven app: it decides which sections and pages people can click in the navigation.

## Maker
It organises navigation into areas, groups and subareas, where subareas usually open a table's views. Apps with more than one area show a switch at the lower left. The app designer lets you edit it visually.

## Architect
The site map defines the tables and pages included in a [[model-driven-app]] and the navigation that users get. Because it is app metadata, it ships in a [[solution]] with the app. Older designers edited it as XML; the modern app designer replaces that.
