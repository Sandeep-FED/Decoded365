## Beginner
AI Builder lets you add ready-made AI skills, such as reading text from a form, to apps and flows without needing data science knowledge.

## Maker
You pick a model type, such as extracting information from documents or classifying text, and use it inside [[power-apps]] or [[power-automate]]. Check which models are currently available in your region before you plan around them.

## Architect
AI Builder models run as a service in an [[environment]] and use capacity, so plan capacity and measure accuracy on your own documents. Related document-focused features also exist elsewhere in Microsoft 365; see [[document-processing]].
