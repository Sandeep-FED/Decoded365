Power Automate

Power PlatformGenerally availableautomation

Also known as: Microsoft Flow

Beginner

Power Automate lets you set up a chain of steps that runs by itself, such as saving email attachments to a folder or asking a manager to approve a request.

Maker

Automations are called flows. A Cloud flow runs online when something happens or on a schedule, and a Desktop flow repeats actions on a Windows computer. Flows use connectors to talk to other services. It used to be called Microsoft Flow, so older articles may say 'Flow' for the whole product.

Architect

Flows belong to an Environment and run under a connection's permissions, so a change of owner, a shared connection or licensing can decide whether a flow keeps running. Move flows with solutions and use data policies to stop risky connector combinations. A model-driven Business process flow is authored in the same designer but is a different thing.
