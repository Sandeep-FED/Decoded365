Log level

SharePointGenerally availableDraftadministration

Draft. This entry was written with AI and has not yet been checked by a person. Treat it as a starting point, and use "Edit this term" below to improve it.

Beginner

A log level is a dial that sets how much detail gets written to a log.

Maker

It is the amount of information that is stored in a log file for a transaction. Levels are represented by numbers or by words, from the most to the least verbose.

Architect

Raising the level adds detail, which helps in troubleshooting but makes logs larger. Lowering it keeps the logs small.
